About Hungry Horrors

Hungry Horrors is a unique roguelite deckbuilder that flips the script on traditional monster encounters. Instead of battling creatures with swords and spells, you'll appease them with delicious traditional dishes from British and Irish cuisine.

Each monster you encounter has distinct food preferences rooted in actual mythology and regional traditions. Serve the right dish, and they'll be satisfied. Serve the wrong one, and well... you'll become the meal.

Build your deck of recipes, manage your ingredients carefully, and master the art of monstrous hospitality. With each run, unlock new dishes, discover creature preferences, and expand your culinary arsenal.

Developed by Clumsy Bear Studio, Hungry Horrors features hand-pixelled art and a bespoke soundtrack that perfectly captures the eerie yet whimsical atmosphere of folklore-inspired cooking adventures.

How to Play

1

Encounter Monsters

Explore and face creatures from British and Irish folklore. Each monster has unique dietary needs and preferences you'll need to discover.

2

Cook Traditional Dishes

Use your deck of recipe cards to prepare dishes. Manage your ingredients wisely and build the perfect meal to satisfy each creature.

3

Feed the Horrors

Serve your dish to the hungry monster. Match their preferences correctly to appease them. Get it wrong, and face the consequences.

4

Upgrade & Retry

Collect loot, unlock new recipes, and upgrade your abilities. Each run makes you stronger for the next attempt. Die, learn, and cook again!

Before your first Hungry Horrors run

Start from the developer's page. Open the official demo using the button above, then choose “Run game” on itch.io. The game runs there; this guide does not host the game files.

Give the download time. In our browser check, the demo reached its welcome screen and main menu after the loading bar completed. That check confirms startup, not a complete playthrough. Loading time depends on your connection and device.

If the game stays blank: keep the official tab open while it loads. If the bar stops progressing, try a current desktop browser. The developer's page also lists downloadable versions if browser play fails.

Learn before chasing combinations. Follow the in-game introduction and read the descriptions of the dishes and the Horror in front of you. This page explains the basic loop; it does not provide a verified recipe tier list or monster preference database.

Hungry Horrors demo FAQ

Where can I play the Hungry Horrors browser demo?

Use Clumsy Bear Studio's official itch.io page and select “Run game”. Our demo button opens that page in a new tab.

Is the free demo the full game?

No. The browser version is a demo. Check the developer's listing for its current contents and Steam for the full game's availability and price.

Why does the demo open on a different website?

itch.io blocked direct embedding when we tested it on this website. Linking to the original developer page avoids a broken game frame and keeps you on the official distribution page.

Why is Hungry Horrors taking a long time to load?

The browser demo downloads game assets before it starts. Wait for the loading bar to finish. If it stops progressing, try another desktop browser or review the download options on the official page.

Is this the official Hungry Horrors website?

No. hungryhorrors.online is an independent fan guide. Clumsy Bear Studio develops Hungry Horrors. Game names and related rights belong to their respective owners.
